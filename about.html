About MaazCalc

Free and Simple Online Calculators

Welcome to MaazCalc

MaazCalc is a free online calculator website created to make everyday calculations simple, fast and accessible. Our goal is to provide useful calculators that anyone can use directly from a web browser without installing additional software.

What We Provide

MaazCalc provides calculators for common academic, financial and everyday calculations. Our current tools include:

  • Percentage Calculator
  • Percentage Increase and Decrease Calculator
  • Marks Percentage Calculator
  • Discount Calculator
  • Profit and Loss Calculator
  • GPA and CGPA Calculator

Our Goal

Our goal is to build a collection of simple and useful online tools that save users time and make calculations easier to understand. We aim to continuously improve MaazCalc and add more calculators based on common user needs.

Simple, Fast and Mobile-Friendly

MaazCalc is designed to work on smartphones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers. The calculators run directly in your browser and are designed to provide quick results without unnecessary steps.

About the Creator

MaazCalc was created by Maaz Gul with the goal of developing practical and easy-to-use online tools for everyday calculations.
