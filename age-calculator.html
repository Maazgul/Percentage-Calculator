How to Calculate Age in Pakistan? – Formula

Age is calculated by subtracting date of birth from current date. The formula is:

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth

The result is expressed in years, months, and days. For example:

  • Born: 15 August 2000
  • Today: 17 October 2026
  • Age: 26 years, 2 months, 2 days

Why Age Calculator is Important in Pakistan?

In Pakistan, age is required for many purposes:

  • Government Jobs (FPSC, PPSC, NTS): Age limit is usually 18-30 years. Age is calculated as on closing date of application.
  • School Admissions: Minimum age is 3-4 years for Play Group, 5 years for Class 1.
  • CNIC (NADRA): Required at 18 years.
  • Passport: Required for all ages (child passport needs both parents).
  • Voting: Legal age is 18 years.
  • Driving License: Legal age is 18 years.

How to Calculate Age Manually?

If you want to calculate your age without a tool, subtract your birth year from the current year. If the current month is before your birth month, subtract one more year. To find the exact months and days, you must calculate based on the number of days in each month and account for leap years (like February 29). How to calculate age in Excel manually?

To calculate age in Excel, use the formula =DATEDIF(birth_date, TODAY(), "Y") for years. For years, months, and days combined, use =DATEDIF(birth_date, TODAY(), "Y") & " years, " & DATEDIF(birth_date, TODAY(), "YM") & " months, " & DATEDIF(birth_date, TODAY(), "MD") & " days".
