What is BMI? – Simple Explanation for Pakistan

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is calculated from your weight and height and tells you whether you are underweight, normal, overweight or obese. It's a quick screening tool used by doctors worldwide, including in Pakistan, to check if a person's weight is in healthy range.

BMI does not measure body fat directly, but it correlates well with body fat for most people. Athletes with high muscle mass may show high BMI but low body fat.

BMI Formula – How to Calculate

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

For Pakistani users who know height in feet & inches:

Height in meters = (Feet × 12 + Inches) × 0.0254

Example: If your height is 5'8" and weight is 70kg:
Height in inches = (5×12) + 8 = 68 inches
Height in meters = 68 × 0.0254 = 1.7272 m
BMI = 70 ÷ (1.7272 × 1.7272) = 23.4 (Normal)

What to Do If Your BMI is Not Normal?

If BMI is high (Overweight or Obese): Reduce 300–500 calories per day, walk 30–45 minutes daily, reduce sugar, white rice, and paratha. Increase vegetables, daal, chicken, fish. Drink 8 glasses of water. Consult a doctor if BMI is above 30.

If BMI is low (Underweight): Eat 5–6 small meals, add healthy calories – nuts, banana, milk, eggs, ghee. Include protein – chicken, fish, daal, chana. Do light strength training. Get checked for anemia or thyroid if weight loss is sudden.

Frequently Asked Questions – BMI Calculator Pakistan

What is BMI and how is it calculated in Pakistan?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. Formula: BMI = weight in kg / (height in meters)². In Pakistan, most people use feet/inches for height, so we first convert to meters (1 foot = 0.3048m). Example: 70kg, 5'8" (1.73m) = 70/(1.73*1.73) = 23.4 Normal weight.

What is normal BMI range for Pakistani men and women?

As per WHO, normal BMI range is 18.5 to 24.9 for both men and women. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25-29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is obese. Some studies suggest South Asians (including Pakistanis) have higher health risks at lower BMI, so ideal range for Pakistanis is 18.5 to 23.

How do I calculate BMI with feet and inches?

Formula: First convert height to inches (feet × 12 + inches), then to meters (inches × 0.0254). Then BMI = weight in kg / (height in meters)². Example: 5'8" = 68 inches = 1.7272m. If weight is 70kg, BMI = 70/(1.7272*1.7272) = 23.4 Normal.

What is a healthy BMI for a 5'5" Pakistani woman?

For 5'5" (165cm) height, healthy BMI is 18.5 to 24.9, which means healthy weight range is 50kg to 68kg. For South Asian women, ideal BMI is 18.5 to 23 (50kg to 62kg). Below 50kg is underweight, above 68kg is overweight.

What is a healthy BMI for a 5'8" Pakistani man?

For 5'8" (173cm) height, healthy BMI is 18.5 to 24.9, which means healthy weight range is 55kg to 75kg. For South Asian men, ideal BMI is 18.5 to 23 (55kg to 69kg). Below 55kg is underweight, above 75kg is overweight.

What is the difference between BMI and body fat?

BMI measures weight relative to height, while body fat % measures actual fat in body. BMI does not distinguish between muscle and fat – muscular athletes may have high BMI but low body fat. For accurate fat measurement, use body fat scale or DEXA scan. BMI is a quick screening tool, not diagnostic.

Is BMI accurate for Pakistani people?

BMI is a general screening tool. Research shows South Asians (including Pakistanis) have 3-5% higher body fat at same BMI compared to Caucasians, and higher risk of diabetes/heart disease. So for Pakistanis, ideal BMI may be 18.5-23. Always consult doctor for personalized advice.

What should I do if my BMI is high?

If BMI is 25+ (overweight) or 30+ (obese), focus on: 1) Reduce 300-500 calories/day, 2) Walk 30-45 minutes daily, 3) Reduce sugar, white rice, paratha, 4) Increase vegetables, daal, chicken, fish, 5) Drink 8 glasses water. Consult a doctor or nutritionist. Avoid crash diets.

What should I do if my BMI is low (underweight)?

If BMI is below 18.5, focus on: 1) Eat 5-6 small meals/day, 2) Add healthy calories – nuts, banana, milk, eggs, ghee, 3) Include protein – chicken, fish, daal, chana, 4) Do light strength training, 5) Get checked for anemia, thyroid, or diabetes. Consult doctor if weight loss is sudden.
