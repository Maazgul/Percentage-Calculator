How to Calculate Discount in PKR? – Formulas

Discount Amount (Bachat) = Original Price × Discount % ÷ 100

Final Price (Aakhri Keemat) = Original Price − Discount Amount

Discount % = (Discount Amount ÷ Original Price) × 100

These formulas work for any shopping in Pakistan. For example, if a product's original price is 5000 PKR and shopkeeper says 20% off for Eid Sale, discount amount is 1000 PKR and you pay 4000 PKR.

Example 2 – Bazaar Bargaining: A shirt original price in Tariq Road Karachi is 3000 PKR, shopkeeper gives 25% discount. Discount = 3000 × 0.25 = 750 PKR. Final = 2250 PKR.

Example 3 – Double Discount (20% + 10%): Many stores offer 20% + extra 10% off. This is NOT 30%. First discount: 5000 PKR → 4000 PKR after 20% off. Second 10% on 4000 = 400 PKR. Final = 3600 PKR. Total saving = 1400 PKR (28% effective).

How do I calculate a discount in PKR?

Formula: Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100. Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount. For example, original price 5000 PKR, 20% off: Discount = 5000*20/100=1000 PKR, Final = 5000-1000=4000 PKR.

How to calculate 30% off in Pakistan?

To calculate 30% off, multiply price by 0.30 to get discount amount. For example, 3000 PKR product with 30% off: Discount = 3000*0.30=900 PKR, Final Price = 2100 PKR. Common in Daraz 11.11 sale and Eid sales.

How to calculate original price after discount?

If you know final price and discount %, original price = Final Price / (1 - Discount%/100). For example, if you paid 4000 PKR after 20% discount, original price = 4000 / 0.80 = 5000 PKR. Useful to check fake discounts.

What is the formula for discount percentage?

Discount Percentage = (Discount Amount / Original Price) * 100. If original price 2000 PKR and discount is 400 PKR, discount % = (400/2000)*100 = 20%.

How to calculate double discount like 20% + 10%?

Double discount is not 30% directly. First apply 20% on original price, then apply 10% on discounted price. For example, 5000 PKR: After 20% = 4000 PKR, after extra 10% on 4000 = 3600 PKR final price. Total effective discount = 28%, not 30%.
