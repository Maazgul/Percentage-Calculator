How to Calculate Discount?

The basic discount formula is: Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100 .

After finding the discount amount, use: Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount .

Example of a Discount Calculation

Suppose an item costs 5,000 and has a 20% discount. The discount amount is 1,000 and the final price is 4,000.
