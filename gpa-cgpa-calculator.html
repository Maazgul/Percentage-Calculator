How to Calculate GPA?

GPA is calculated by multiplying each subject's grade point by its credit hours, adding all weighted grade points, and dividing the total by the total number of credit hours.

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours)

Example of GPA Calculation

Suppose you have two subjects. Subject 1 has a grade point of 3.0 with 3 credit hours, while Subject 2 has a grade point of 4.0 with 3 credit hours.

GPA = [(3.0 × 3) + (4.0 × 3)] ÷ (3 + 3) = 3.50

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents the combined academic performance across multiple semesters or academic periods. The exact CGPA calculation method can vary depending on the educational institution.
