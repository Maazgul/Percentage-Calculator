How to Calculate GPA? – Official Formula for Pakistan

GPA is calculated by multiplying each subject's grade point by its credit hours, adding all weighted points, and dividing by total credit hours. This is the official formula used in UCP, FAST, NUST, and all HEC recognized universities in Pakistan.

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours)

Example for UCP / FAST University Student: Suppose you have 3 subjects in a semester: Data Structures – Grade Point 3.7 (A) with 3 credit hours, Calculus – Grade Point 3.0 (B+) with 3 credit hours, Islamic Studies – Grade Point 4.0 (A+) with 2 credit hours. Calculation: (3.7×3) + (3.0×3) + (4.0×2) = 11.1 + 9 + 8 = 28.1. Total credits = 3+3+2 = 8. GPA = 28.1 ÷ 8 = 3.51 GPA.

How to Calculate CGPA in University? – Complete Guide

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the average of all your semester GPAs combined, weighted by credit hours. To calculate CGPA in university in Pakistan:

  1. Multiply each subject's grade point by its credit hours.
  2. Add all weighted points from all semesters.
  3. Divide by total credit hours of all semesters.

CGPA = Σ(All Semesters Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(All Semesters Credit Hours)

Example: If you're in 8th semester of BS Computer Science in UCP or NUST, your CGPA is the weighted average of all 8 semesters. Simply add all subjects from all semesters in our calculator and click Calculate – it will show your CGPA.

What is CGPA? – Complete Guide for Pakistan

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents combined academic performance across multiple semesters or entire degree (4 years BS, 2 years MSc). For example, if you are in 8th semester of BS Computer Science in UCP or NUST, your CGPA is average of all 8 semesters weighted by credits.

CGPA = (Sum of All Semesters Weighted Points) ÷ (Total Credits of All Semesters)
