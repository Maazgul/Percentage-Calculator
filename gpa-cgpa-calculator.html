How to Calculate GPA? – Official Formula for Pakistan

GPA is calculated by multiplying each subject's grade point by its credit hours, adding all weighted points, and dividing by total credit hours. This is the official formula used in FAST, NUST, and all HEC recognized universities in Pakistan.

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours)

Example for FAST University Student: Suppose you have 3 subjects in a semester: Data Structures – Grade Point 3.7 (A) with 3 credit hours, Calculus – Grade Point 3.0 (B+) with 3 credit hours, Islamic Studies – Grade Point 4.0 (A+) with 2 credit hours. Calculation: (3.7×3) + (3.0×3) + (4.0×2) = 11.1 + 9 + 8 = 28.1. Total credits = 3+3+2 = 8. GPA = 28.1 ÷ 8 = 3.51 GPA.

What is CGPA? – Complete Guide for Pakistan

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents combined academic performance across multiple semesters or entire degree (4 years BS, 2 years MSc). For example, if you are in 8th semester of BS Computer Science in NUST, your CGPA is average of all 8 semesters weighted by credits.

CGPA = (Sum of All Semesters Weighted Points) ÷ (Total Credits of All Semesters)

How to Convert CGPA to Percentage in Pakistan?

Many Pakistani students need to convert CGPA to percentage for jobs or foreign admissions. Common formulas:

Formula 1 (Most Common): Percentage = CGPA × 25

Formula 2: Percentage = (CGPA ÷ 4.0) × 100

Example: 3.2 CGPA = 3.2 × 25 = 80% Percentage. Or (3.2/4)*100 = 80%.
Note: Some universities like UET have slightly different formulas (e.g., Percentage = (CGPA - 0.5) * 20). Always check your university handbook. Our Marks Percentage Calculator can help convert.

Frequently Asked Questions – GPA Pakistan

What is a GPA in Pakistani universities?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. In Pakistan universities like FAST, NUST, LUMS, UET, Karachi University, Punjab University, GPA is calculated on 4.0 scale. It represents average of grade points weighted by credit hours per semester.

How is GPA calculated for FAST, NUST, UET?

GPA Formula: Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours). Example: Subject 1: 3.5 GPA × 3 credits = 10.5, Subject 2: 4.0 × 3 credits = 12, Total = 22.5 ÷ 6 credits = 3.75 GPA. This method is used in all Pakistani universities.

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It is average of all semester GPAs combined. For example, if you have 8 semesters in BS, CGPA = (Sum of all semester GPAs weighted by credits) / Total Credits of all semesters.

How to convert CGPA to percentage in Pakistan?

Many Pakistani universities use: Percentage = CGPA × 25. Or (CGPA / 4.0) × 100. Example: 3.2 CGPA = 80%. Check our Marks Calculator for conversion. Some universities have different formulas.

What is 4.0 GPA grading scale in Pakistan?

In Pakistan: 4.0 = A+ (90-100%), 3.7-3.99 = A (80-89%), 3.0-3.69 = B+ (70-79%), 2.0-2.99 = B (60-69%), 1.0-1.99 = C (50-59%), below 1.0 = Fail. Used in FAST, NUST, LUMS, UET.

Can I calculate GPA using different credit hours like 1, 2, 3, 4?

Yes, our calculator supports any credit hours (1, 2, 3, 4 credits) as used in Pakistani universities. Lab courses often have 1 credit, theory courses 3 or 4 credits.

What is good GPA in Pakistan universities?

Good GPA in Pakistan: 3.5+ is Excellent (Top, scholarships), 3.0-3.49 is Good (First Division), 2.5-2.99 is Satisfactory, below 2.0 is low. For jobs, most companies require at least 2.5 to 3.0 CGPA.

How to calculate semester GPA and overall CGPA?

Semester GPA = (Sum of Grade Point × Credits for that semester) / Total Credits that semester. CGPA = (Sum of All Semesters Weighted Points) / Total Credits of all semesters.

Is GPA and CGPA same?

No. GPA is for one semester only. CGPA is Cumulative GPA for all semesters combined (overall degree GPA). For example, 1st semester GPA 3.5, 2nd GPA 3.2, then CGPA after 2 semesters is weighted average.
