How to Calculate GPA – Official Formula

GPA is calculated by multiplying each subject's grade point by its credit hours, adding all weighted points, and dividing by total credit hours. This is the official formula used in all HEC-recognized universities in Pakistan.

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours)

Example: Data Structures – 3.7 GP × 3 credits = 11.1, Calculus – 3.0 GP × 3 credits = 9.0, Islamic Studies – 4.0 GP × 2 credits = 8.0. Total = 28.1 ÷ 8 credits = 3.51 GPA.

How to Calculate CGPA in University

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the weighted average of all semester GPAs. To calculate:

  1. Multiply each subject's grade point by its credit hours.
  2. Add all weighted points from all semesters.
  3. Divide by total credit hours of all semesters.
CGPA = Σ(All Semesters Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(All Semesters Credit Hours)

3.95 CGPA to Percentage

Use the standard formula: Percentage = CGPA × 25. So, 3.95 CGPA = 3.95 × 25 = 98.75%. This is an excellent CGPA equivalent to A+ grade in Pakistani universities.

Frequently Asked Questions – GPA & CGPA Pakistan

What is a GPA in Pakistani universities?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is calculated on a 4.0 scale. It represents the average of grade points weighted by credit hours per semester.

How is GPA calculated for UCP, FAST, NUST, UET?

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours). This method is used in all Pakistani universities.

What is UCP GPA Calculator?

How to calculate 3.95 CGPA to percentage?

Percentage = CGPA × 25. So 3.95 CGPA = 3.95 × 25 = 98.75%.

How to calculate CGPA in university in Pakistan?

Multiply each subject's grade point by credit hours, add all weighted points, and divide by total credit hours of all semesters.

What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the average of all semester GPAs combined, weighted by credit hours.

What is a good GPA in Pakistan?

3.5+ is Excellent, 3.0–3.49 is Good (First Division), 2.5–2.99 is Satisfactory, below 2.0 is low.

Is GPA and CGPA the same?

No. GPA is for one semester; CGPA is the cumulative GPA for all semesters combined.
