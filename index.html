Frequently Asked Questions

What is a percentage calculator?

A percentage calculator helps you quickly calculate percentages, percentage changes, marks percentages and other percentage-related calculations.

How do I calculate percentage?

Use the formula: Percentage = (Obtained Value ÷ Total Value) × 100. For example, 425 out of 500 is 85%.

How do I calculate marks percentage?

Enter your obtained marks and total marks in the Marks Percentage Calculator. The calculator automatically calculates your percentage.

How do I calculate a discount?

Enter the original price and discount percentage. MaazCalc calculates the discount amount and the final price.

How do I calculate profit or loss?

Enter the cost price and selling price. The calculator shows whether you have made a profit or loss and calculates the percentage.

How is GPA calculated?

GPA is calculated using the weighted average of grade points and credit hours: GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours).
