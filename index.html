How to Calculate Percentage?

The basic percentage formula is: Percentage = (Obtained Value ÷ Total Value) × 100. For example, if you score 425 marks out of 500, your percentage is 85%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a percentage calculator?

A percentage calculator helps you quickly calculate percentages, percentage changes, marks percentages and other percentage-related calculations for students and everyday use.

How do I calculate marks percentage?

To calculate your marks percentage, use the formula: (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100. For example, if you scored 850 out of 1100 marks, your percentage will be (850 ÷ 1100) × 100 = 77.27%. Our Marks Percentage Calculator is specially designed for Pakistani students of Matric, Intermediate, O-Level and A-Level to quickly calculate their annual result percentage without any manual formula.

How do I calculate a discount?

Discount is calculated using the formula: Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount % ÷ 100) and Final Price = Original Price - Discount Amount. For example, if a shirt costs Rs. 2000 and has a 25% discount, the discount is 500 and you will pay Rs. 1500. MaazCalc automatically calculates both the saved amount and the final price you have to pay after discount.

How do I calculate profit or loss?

Profit or Loss is found by comparing Cost Price (CP) and Selling Price (SP). If SP is greater than CP, you have a profit. If CP is greater than SP, you have a loss. The formula is: Profit % = ((SP - CP) ÷ CP) × 100. For example, if you bought a product for Rs. 1000 and sold it for Rs. 1200, your profit is Rs. 200 and profit percentage is 20%. Our calculator instantly tells you profit or loss with percentage.

How is GPA calculated?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is calculated using the weighted average method. The formula is: GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours). For example, if you have two subjects, one with 3 credit hours and 4.0 grade point, and another with 3 credit hours and 3.0 grade point, your GPA will be ((4.0×3)+(3.0×3)) ÷ 6 = 3.5.
