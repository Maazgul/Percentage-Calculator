How to Calculate Marks Percentage?

The marks percentage formula is: Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100.

For example, if you obtain 425 marks out of 500:

(425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%

How to Calculate Percentage Increase?

To calculate percentage increase, subtract the old value from the new value, divide the difference by the old value, and multiply by 100.

Percentage Increase = ((New Value − Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100

How to Calculate Percentage Decrease?

To calculate percentage decrease, subtract the new value from the old value, divide the difference by the old value, and multiply by 100.

Percentage Decrease = ((Old Value − New Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100
