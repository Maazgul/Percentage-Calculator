How to Calculate Marks Percentage? – Complete Guide for Pakistani Students

Marks percentage calculation is essential for every student in Pakistan, whether you are in Matric (9th, 10th), Intermediate (FSc, FA, ICS, I.Com), O-Level, A-Level, or University. Our Marks Percentage Calculator uses the official formula accepted by all BISE boards in Pakistan including BISE Lahore, Karachi, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Multan, Sargodha, Rawalpindi, and FBISE Islamabad.

Official Formula:

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example 1 – Matric (1100 Marks): If you obtained 950 marks out of 1100 in Matric, your percentage is (950 ÷ 1100) × 100 = 86.36%. This is A+ Grade.

Example 2 – 9th Class (550 Marks): If you got 420 out of 550 in 9th class, percentage = (420 ÷ 550) × 100 = 76.36%.

Example 3 – FSc Pre-Medical: If you got 900 out of 1100 in FSc, percentage = (900/1100)*100 = 81.81% which is required for MBBS admission in many medical colleges in Pakistan.

Frequently Asked Questions – Marks Percentage

How do I calculate marks percentage for Matric in Pakistan?

For Matric (BISE) in Pakistan, formula is (Obtained Marks / Total Marks) * 100. If you got 950 out of 1100, percentage is (950/1100)*100 = 86.36%. This applies to all BISE boards like Lahore, Karachi, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, FBISE Islamabad.

What is the formula for marks percentage?

The universal formula is (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100 = Percentage. For example, 425 out of 500 equals (425/500)*100 = 85%.

How to calculate percentage for O-Level and A-Level?

For O-Level/A-Level (Cambridge) in Pakistan, you can still use same formula: (Obtained Marks / Total Marks)*100. However, Cambridge also uses grades (A*, A, B). For example, 80% and above is generally A* grade.

What is 80% marks in Pakistan grading system?

In Pakistan BISE system, 80% and above is A+ Grade (Excellent), 70-79% is A Grade, 60-69% is B Grade, 50-59% is C Grade, 40-49% is D Grade, below 40% is Fail.

Can I use this calculator for university CGPA percentage?

This calculator is for marks percentage. For university CGPA to percentage conversion, many Pakistani universities use (CGPA / 4)*100 or (CGPA * 25). For example, 3.2 CGPA = 80%. Check our dedicated GPA & CGPA Calculator.

How to calculate marks percentage for Intermediate FSc?

Same formula for FSc, FA, ICS, I.Com: (Obtained Marks / Total Marks)*100. If total is 1100 and you got 900, percentage is 81.81% which is used for university admissions in Pakistan.

Is this marks calculator accurate for BISE Lahore, Karachi, Gujranwala boards?

Yes, 100% accurate for all BISE boards in Pakistan including Lahore, Karachi, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Multan, Sargodha, Rawalpindi, and FBISE Islamabad, as well as for CBSE boards.

How to calculate percentage if total marks are 550?

For 9th class where total is 550, formula is same: (Obtained/550)*100. For example, 450 out of 550 = 81.81%.

What is the difference between percentage and CGPA?

Percentage is (Obtained/Total)*100 out of 100%. CGPA is Cumulative Grade Point Average out of 4.0 scale used in universities. You can convert CGPA to percentage by multiplying by 25 in Pakistan.
