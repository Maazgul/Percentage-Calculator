How to Calculate Percentage – Formulas with Pakistani Examples

Understanding percentage formulas is essential for exams and daily life. Here are the 3 main formulas:

1. Basic Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example for Pakistan: You got 850 marks out of 1100 in BISE Lahore Matric exam. Percentage = (850 ÷ 1100) × 100 = 77.27% (B+ Grade). Our Marks Percentage Calculator gives this with grade automatically.

2. Percentage Increase Formula

Increase % = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: Your shop sales in Karachi increased from 50,000 PKR to 65,000 PKR. Increase = ((65000−50000)÷50000)×100 = 30% growth.

3. Percentage Decrease / Discount Formula

Decrease % = ((Original Value − New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: A jacket original price is 4000 PKR, sale price 3000 PKR. Discount = ((4000−3000)÷4000)×100 = 25% OFF. Try our Discount Calculator in PKR for final price after discount.

Frequently Asked Questions – Percentage Calculator Pakistan

How do I calculate 10% of a number?

Divide 10 by 100 which is 0.1 and multiply the result by the number. For example, 10% of 200 is 0.1 * 200 = 20. This is very easy to calculate and useful for quick mental math and 10% discounts in Pakistan.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value and multiply by 100. Example: Price from 1000 PKR to 1200 PKR, increase = ((1200-1000)/1000)*100 = 20% increase.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new value from the original value, divide by the original value and multiply by 100. If a mobile price drops from 2000 PKR to 1500 PKR, decrease = ((2000-1500)/2000)*100 = 25% discount.

What is the formula for percentage?

Basic formula is (Part / Whole) * 100 = Percentage. Or to find X% of a number: (Percentage / 100) * Number. For example, to find 20% of 500: (20/100)*500 = 100.

How to calculate marks percentage for Matric and Intermediate in Pakistan?

For all BISE boards in Pakistan (Lahore, Karachi, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Islamabad), formula is (Obtained Marks / Total Marks) * 100. If you got 950 out of 1100 in Matric, percentage = (950/1100)*100 = 86.36%. Use our dedicated Marks Percentage Calculator for accurate grades.

What is the difference between percent and percentage?

Percent (%) means per hundred and is used with a specific number (e.g., 50%). Percentage is a general term for a proportion out of 100 without a specific number (e.g., 'a large percentage of students passed'). In math, both refer to same concept.

How to calculate discount percentage in Pakistan?

If original price is 3000 PKR and sale price is 2400 PKR, discount % = ((3000-2400)/3000)*100 = 20%. Our Discount Calculator in PKR helps you find final price after discount easily.

How to find 100% of a number?

100% of any number is the number itself. Formula: (100/100) * Number = Number. So 100% of 750 is 750. This is useful to understand that 100% means whole or complete.
