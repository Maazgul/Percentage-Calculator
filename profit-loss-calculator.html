What is Profit and Loss? – Guide for Pakistan

Profit and loss (Nafa aur Nuqsan) are the most important concepts for every shopkeeper, small business owner, Daraz seller, and student in Pakistan. When your selling price (Frokht Keemat) is higher than cost price (Kharid Keemat), you make profit (Nafa). When selling price is lower than cost price, you face loss (Nuqsan). Understanding profit percentage helps you set correct prices for your shop in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, or online business.

Profit Formulas in PKR

Profit (Nafa) = Selling Price − Cost Price

Profit % = (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Example for Pakistani Shopkeeper: You buy a shirt for 1000 PKR from Shah Alam Market Lahore (cost price) and sell it for 1250 PKR in your shop (selling price). Profit = 1250 - 1000 = 250 PKR. Profit % = (250 ÷ 1000) × 100 = 25% profit margin. This is good profit for clothes business.

Loss Formulas in PKR

Loss (Nuqsan) = Cost Price − Selling Price

Loss % = (Loss ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Example: You bought mobile covers for 1000 PKR stock, but due to low demand you sell for 800 PKR. Loss = 1000 - 800 = 200 PKR. Loss % = (200 ÷ 1000) × 100 = 20% loss.

Good Profit Margin for Small Business in Pakistan

For small businesses in Pakistan, good profit margin depends on product type:

  • Kirana / Grocery Store: 10% – 20% margin
  • Clothes / Garments: 30% – 60% margin
  • Mobile Accessories: 40% – 100% margin
  • Daraz / Online Selling: 15% – 35% after Daraz fees
  • Food / Restaurant: 50% – 80% margin

Frequently Asked Questions – Profit & Loss Pakistan

How is profit calculated in Pakistan?

Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price in PKR. Example: Buy for 1000 PKR, Sell for 1250 PKR, Profit = 250 PKR.

How is loss calculated?

Loss = Cost Price - Selling Price. If cost 1000 PKR and sell 800 PKR, loss = 200 PKR. Common during sale season.

How is profit percentage calculated?

Profit % = (Profit / Cost Price) * 100. Example: Cost 1000, Sell 1250, Profit 250, Profit % = 25% margin.

How is loss percentage calculated?

Loss % = (Loss / Cost Price) * 100. Example: Cost 1000, Sell 800, Loss 200, Loss % = 20%.

What is good profit margin for small business in Pakistan?

Good margin is 15% to 40% for small businesses. Grocery 10-20%, clothes 30-50%, mobile accessories 40-100% as per Pakistani bazaar trends.

How to calculate profit for Daraz sellers?

Daraz sellers: Total Cost = Product Cost + Daraz Commission + Delivery + Packaging. Profit = Customer Payment - Total Cost. Calculate profit % after all costs using our tool.

How to calculate profit if there is discount?

First calculate selling price after discount using our Discount Calculator, then use that as selling price in profit calculator. Example: Cost 2000, Sell 3000, 10% discount = 2700 final sell, profit = 700 PKR (35%).

What is difference between profit and profit margin?

Profit is amount in PKR (250 PKR). Profit margin is % (25%). Margin helps compare different products profitability.

How to avoid loss in business?

Always set selling price higher than total cost including rent, electricity, delivery. Keep margin at least 15% for Pakistani businesses to cover expenses and make profit.
