What is Profit and Loss?

Profit and loss are commonly used to measure the result of buying and selling goods or products. When the selling price is higher than the cost price, you make a profit. When the selling price is lower than the cost price, you experience a loss.

Profit Formula

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price

Profit percentage is calculated using: Profit Percentage = (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Loss Formula

Loss = Cost Price − Selling Price

Loss percentage is calculated using: Loss Percentage = (Loss ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Example of Profit

If the cost price of a product is 1,000 and the selling price is 1,250, the profit is 250. The profit percentage is 25%.

Example of Loss

If the cost price is 1,000 and the selling price is 800, the loss is 200. The loss percentage is 20%.
