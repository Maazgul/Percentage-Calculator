What is Zakat? – Meaning & Importance

Zakat (زکوٰۃ) literally means "purification" and "growth". In Islam, it is an obligatory annual charity on wealth, given to the poor and needy. It is the 3rd pillar of Islam after Shahadah and Salah. The Quran mentions Zakat over 30 times, often alongside Salah.

Zakat purifies wealth, reduces inequality, and helps the poor. In Pakistan, Zakat is collected by the government under the Zakat and Ushr Ordinance 1980 on certain bank accounts, but many Muslims also calculate and pay Zakat personally.

Zakat Formula – How to Calculate

Zakat = 2.5% × (Total Zakatable Wealth − Immediate Debts)

Zakat becomes obligatory if:

  • You are an adult Muslim
  • Your wealth exceeds Nisab
  • One lunar year (Hawl) has passed on that wealth

Zakat on Gold in Pakistan – Detailed Guide

Zakat on gold is 2.5% of the current market value of all gold you own, if total wealth exceeds Nisab and one year has passed. This includes jewelry (worn or stored), coins, bars, and gold saved for investment.

Example: If you own 10 tola gold and 1 tola = 200,000 PKR, total = 2,000,000 PKR. Zakat = 2.5% × 2,000,000 = 50,000 PKR.

Note: Hanafi fiqh (majority in Pakistan) requires Zakat on worn gold jewelry as well. Some Maliki scholars exempt jewelry in regular use. Consult your scholar.

Zakat on Silver in Pakistan

Silver Nisab is much lower than gold, so many Pakistanis use silver Nisab to determine Zakat obligation. If you own more than 52.5 tola silver (612.36g), Zakat is due at 2.5% of value.

Zakat on Cash, Bank Balance, and Salary

All cash – whether in hand, savings account, current account, fixed deposit, or foreign currency – is zakatable if total wealth exceeds Nisab. You calculate its value on your Zakat date (the day one lunar year completes) and pay 2.5%.

Salary itself is not separately zakatable, but if you've saved it and it stays for one year, it becomes zakatable.

Zakat on Business & Daraz Sellers

Shopkeepers, traders, and Daraz/online sellers in Pakistan must pay Zakat on business stock (inventory) at market value on the Zakat date. Fixed assets (shop building, delivery van, machinery) are not zakatable. Only the goods for sale are.

Example: A mobile shop with 500,000 PKR inventory at market value pays Zakat = 2.5% × 500,000 = 12,500 PKR (if total wealth > Nisab).

Zakat on Investments – Shares, Mutual Funds, Crypto

Shares and mutual funds are zakatable at their current market value if held as investment. Crypto (Bitcoin, etc.) is debated by scholars – many consider it an asset and require Zakat on market value.

When to Pay Zakat in Pakistan?

Zakat is due once a lunar year (Hawl) has passed on your wealth. Many Muslims choose Ramadan for extra reward, but it can be paid anytime. You can pick your own Zakat date (e.g., 1st Ramadan, or the date when your wealth first crossed Nisab).

Frequently Asked Questions – Zakat Calculator Pakistan

What is Zakat and who must pay it in Pakistan?

Zakat is the 3rd pillar of Islam – an obligatory annual charity of 2.5% on a Muslim's zakatable wealth if it exceeds the Nisab threshold and has been held for one lunar year (Hawl). In Pakistan, every adult Muslim who owns wealth above Nisab must pay Zakat, whether on gold, silver, cash, bank balance, business stock, or investments.

What is Nisab for Zakat in Pakistan 2026?

Nisab is the minimum threshold of wealth upon which Zakat becomes obligatory. In Pakistan, Nisab is based on either: (1) Silver: 612.36 grams (approx 52.5 tola), or (2) Gold: 87.48 grams (approx 7.5 tola). Most scholars recommend using silver Nisab as it benefits more poor people. As of 2026, silver Nisab is approximately 200,000+ PKR.

How is Zakat calculated on gold in Pakistan?

Zakat on gold is 2.5% of the current market value of gold you own if it exceeds Nisab (7.5 tola / 87.48 grams) and one year has passed. Example: If you own 10 tola gold and 1 tola = 200,000 PKR, total value = 2,000,000 PKR, Zakat = 2.5% × 2,000,000 = 50,000 PKR.

How is Zakat calculated on silver?

Zakat on silver is 2.5% of current value if you own more than Nisab (52.5 tola / 612.36 grams) and one year has passed. Silver Nisab is lower than gold, so if you own silver above 52.5 tola, Zakat becomes obligatory.

Is Zakat applicable on cash and bank balance in Pakistan?

Yes. Zakat is 2.5% on all cash in hand, bank accounts (current, savings, fixed deposits), foreign currency, and money lent to others (receivables) if total exceeds Nisab and one lunar year has passed. This is the most common form of Zakat in Pakistan.

How is Zakat calculated on business stock and inventory?

Zakat on business stock (inventory) is 2.5% of its current market value on your Zakat date, if the total zakatable wealth exceeds Nisab. This applies to shopkeepers, traders, and Daraz sellers in Pakistan. Fixed assets (shop building, machinery) are not zakatable, but the goods for sale are.

Is Zakat applicable on gold jewelry that is worn regularly?

According to the majority of scholars (Hanafi, Hanbali), Zakat is obligatory on all gold and silver, whether worn regularly or stored, if it exceeds Nisab. Some scholars (Maliki) exempt jewelry in regular use. In Pakistan, most people follow Hanafi fiqh, so Zakat on worn jewelry is generally required.

When should Zakat be paid in Pakistan?

Zakat is due once a lunar year (Hawl) has passed on your wealth. Many Muslims in Pakistan pay Zakat in Ramadan for extra reward, but it can be paid any time of the year. The important thing is that one full lunar year has passed since your wealth reached Nisab.

Can I deduct debts and loans before calculating Zakat?

Yes. According to most scholars, you can deduct immediate debts (that are due now), unpaid bills, and liabilities from your total zakatable wealth. However, long-term loans (like 20-year house mortgage) are usually not deducted.
